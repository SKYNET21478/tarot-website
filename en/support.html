Frequently Asked Questions

Q. I paid, but the premium features are still locked.

Close the app completely, open it again and tap "Restore Purchase". Also check that you're using the same Google account you paid with.

Q. I reinstalled the app and premium is gone.

Sign in with the Google account you bought it with and tap "Restore Purchase" to unlock it again.

Q. I'd like a refund.

Refunds follow Google Play's refund policy and can be requested from your Google Play order history. See Google Play Help for the steps.

Q. The spoken reading doesn't play.

Make sure a text-to-speech (TTS) engine with an English voice is installed and your sound is on. You can also try a different TTS engine in your phone's settings.

Q. How do I delete my history?

Tap "Clear History" in the app, or uninstall the app; either removes the history stored on your device.

Q. Are there ads?

No. The app is ad-free so you can focus on your cards and readings.

Q. Can I switch between English and Korean?

The app follows your phone's language: Korean on Korean phones, English everywhere else.
