Tarot Fortune Privacy Policy

Effective: November 5, 2026 · Last updated: November 25, 2026

SKYNET2 (the "developer") values the privacy of everyone who uses the Tarot Fortune – Wheel of Fate app (the "app"), called "타로운세 - 운명의 수레바퀴" in Korean. This policy explains how information is handled. If this English version and the Korean version differ, the Korean version prevails.

1. Personal Information We Collect

The developer does not collect any information that can identify you, such as your name, contact details, location or account information. The app has no sign-up and contains no advertising or analytics tools.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

While you use the app, the following information is stored only on your device. It is never sent to the developer's servers.

  • Your reading history: questions you typed, cards drawn and your notes
  • App settings such as music, voice, card deck and language choices
  • Whether you have bought the premium version (to check your purchase)

You can delete this information with "Clear History" in the app, and it is removed when you uninstall the app.

Tapping the website, Privacy Policy or Terms of Use links in the app opens this website in your phone's browser. Tapping a link does not send your history or settings anywhere.

3. Payments

Premium purchases are processed through Google Play's billing system. Card numbers and other payment details are handled by Google; the developer never receives them. Google's privacy policy applies to payment information.

4. Spoken Readings

Spoken readings use the text-to-speech (TTS) engine installed on your device. The text is processed by that engine (for example, Samsung or Google), and how it is handled may vary by engine. The developer does not collect what is read aloud.

5. Sharing

When you tap "Share", your reading is passed as text to the app you choose (such as a messenger). Sharing happens only when you start it, and nothing is sent to the developer.

6. Permissions the App Uses

  • Vibration: to give a short vibration when you draw cards.
  • Internet, network state and billing (Google Play Billing): to process and verify premium purchases.

7. Sharing with Third Parties

The developer does not provide or sell your information to any third party.

8. Children

The app does not collect personal information from any age group. If a child under 14 uses the app, we recommend a parent's help.

9. Website Review Board

This section applies only to the "Reviews" page of this website, not to the app. The app is not connected to the review board.

  • When you post a review, the nickname, star rating, review text and time you post it are stored. No email address, phone number or other information is collected.
  • Reviews are stored with Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) and, after a check, published on the review page for anyone to see.
  • Please do not include personal information about yourself or others in a review.
  • To delete a review you wrote, email us the nickname and date. We will delete it after checking.

10. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will let you know through the app store listing or this page.
