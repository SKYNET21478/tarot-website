About the App

Tarot Fortune uses all 78 tarot cards to help you sort through your thoughts. Today's Reading draws three cards with a single tap, and the 3-Card Question reads the situation, advice and outcome for anything you ask.

  • Completely ad-free. No banners or video ads, even in the free version.
  • Free: Today's Reading, the 3-Card Question, meanings for every card, sound effects, and your reading history and notes.
  • Readings fit your question: the app picks up its topic (love, work, money, health or your own path) and the kind of question you ask. Reversed cards are read by their nature, so heavy cards show something easing.
  • Premium (one-time purchase, not a subscription): the full reading, more spreads such as Past · Present · Future, Two Choices and the Celtic Cross, three decks (Classic · Marseille · Antique), three music tracks (Mystic Orchestra · Harp · Piano) and spoken readings.
  • Your questions and history stay on your phone. See the Privacy Policy for details.
